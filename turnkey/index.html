TURNKEY

A dungeon that is a cube. You read one flat face at a time — and everything that lines up, touches.

NO DEAD PIXELS
VAULT I

HOW IT WORKS

Four rules. There is no fifth.

👆
Tap a tile to walk there. Only pale bone tiles are decks you can stand on. Dark ones are bedrock, and gaps are nothing at all.
↔
Drag to turn the cube. Past halfway it commits, short of it springs back. The four ticks above your thumb say which turns have footing on the far side.
◫
Depth is discarded. Everything in a column collapses to the nearest solid thing. Two decks far apart in the cube are neighbours on screen, and you may walk between them.
▦
Brightness is distance. A pale tile is near, a dim one is far. The hairlines between them mark a drop you are allowed to ignore.
◈
Keys and doors can be buried behind bedrock. If something is missing, it is behind you in an axis you are not looking down. Turn.
