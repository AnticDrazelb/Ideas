A NEW THING IN THE ROCK

PLATES

Everything you know how to do is about to be worth less.

⊘
THE CUBE TURNS INSIDE OUTEvery deck becomes stone. Every wall becomes floor. Nothing moves — only what you may stand on.
⏱
IT LASTS FIVE SECONDSThen the rock springs back to how it was carved and puts you back on the nearest plate. Running out of time costs you the walk, never the cube. Plan the line before you press it.
⟲
TAP THE PLATE UNDER YOU TO PRESS IT AGAINA plate is a door between two versions of the same cube, and the way out is usually only in one of them. You can always go back through.
◎
IT ALWAYS GIVES FOOTINGCut clean through the rock, visible from every face. While you stand on a plate, every turn is legal — and the clock cannot throw you off one.
