TURNKEY

A dungeon that is a cube. You read one flat face at a time — and everything that lines up, touches.

NO DEAD PIXELS
VAULT I

HOW IT WORKS

Four rules. There is no fifth.

👆
Tap a tile to walk there. Only pale bone tiles are decks you can stand on. Dark ones are bedrock, and gaps are nothing at all.
↔
Drag to turn the cube. Past halfway it commits, short of it springs back. The four ticks above your thumb say which turns have footing on the far side.
◫
Depth is discarded. Everything in a column collapses to the nearest solid thing. Two decks far apart in the cube are neighbours on screen, and you may walk between them.
▦
Brightness is distance. A pale tile is near, a dim one is far. The hairlines between them mark a drop you are allowed to ignore.
◈
Keys and doors can be buried behind bedrock. If something is missing, it is behind you in an axis you are not looking down. Turn.
⊘
Plates rewrite the cube. Step on one and every deck becomes stone and every wall becomes floor — the same cube, a different place. Step on it again to put it back. They appear from cube 20.
⟲
A plate is cut clean through the rock, so you can see it from every face — and it always gives you footing, which means every turn is legal while you stand on one. Plates are pivots.
A NEW THING IN THE ROCK

PLATES

Everything you know how to do is about to be worth less.

⊘
Step on a plate and the cube turns inside out. Every deck becomes stone. Every wall you have been walking around becomes floor. The geometry does not move — only what you may stand on.
⟲
Step on it again and it goes back. The plate is a door between two versions of the same cube, and the way out is usually only reachable from one of them.
◎
A plate is cut clean through the rock, so it is visible from every face however deep it sits — and it always gives footing. While you stand on a plate, every turn is legal.
